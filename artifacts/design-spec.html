Design Rules

Do

  • Use 1px borders for all surface separations.
  • Maintain pure black (#000000) for base backgrounds.
  • Prioritize Geist Mono for any numerical or system data.
  • Apply subtle backdrop-blur on all overlay components.

Don't

  • Avoid large drop shadows; use borders or elevation shifts.
  • Do not use saturated background colors.
  • Avoid rounded corners larger than 12px for technical UI.
  • Don't mix more than 3 distinct font weights.
